The Problem

Multi-location advertising is broken by design

Running local ads manually across 50, 100, or 500 stores isn't a workflow problem — it's a structural one. And it's costing you leads every day.

BRAND

Generic campaigns, zero local relevance

National ads reaching local audiences with irrelevant offers convert poorly. Local intent demands local messaging — at every pin code.

SCALE

Manual creative production doesn't scale

Creating unique creatives per store means weeks of design work, huge agency costs, and campaigns that are outdated before they go live.

ATTR

No attribution across locations

You can't tell which store's campaign drove which customer. So you can't optimize, you can't scale what works, and you can't justify the spend.
